Robotics

Autonomous inspection first. Manipulation later.

Aerosyn is designed around the realistic first robot: a fixed vehicle-inspection station that observes, reasons, reports, and creates the data foundation for future service robotics.

Phase 1 boundary

No wrench-turning until the inspection station proves value.

Robot arms, force control, and arbitrary repair are hard. The first product avoids that risk and focuses on a bounded task with clear customer value: consistent vehicle inspection.

  • Computer vision for exterior and underbody condition capture.
  • OBD-II / J2534 context and VIN-aware reasoning.
  • Advisor review before customer-facing or safety-critical action.
  • Structured data for diagnostics, warranty evidence, fleet readiness, and later robotics.
