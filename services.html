Robotics

Make the physical system concrete.

Robotics work starts by defining what exists in the real world: movement, sensing, space, safety, compute, power, materials, fixtures, users, and failure modes.

  • Prototype briefs and technical requirements.
  • Sensor, edge compute, and data-capture planning.
  • CAD, fabrication, and advanced manufacturing support paths.
  • Human approval and safety boundaries before automation claims.

AI R&D

Use AI where it makes the system easier to build, run, or understand.

AI is part of the R&D stack, not the whole story. It can help structure documents, retrieve knowledge, reason over workflows, generate operator packets, and accelerate prototype loops.
