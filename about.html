About

A consulting firm. It's in the name.

Schubert Consulting LLC is a Michigan consulting company founded by Ian Schubert. We help people and companies with technology — the full spectrum of IT, with deep specialization in applied AI, robotics, and automation.

What We Are

Expertise for hire, not a product to sell.

We're not a startup pitching an app, and we're not raising money. We're consultants. Clients hire us to assess, advise, build, and hand off — and because we have no product to push, our advice is independent by construction.

  • Foundation

    Decades of practical IT.

    Computers, infrastructure, networks, automation, and operations experience shape the firm's bias toward systems that actually work in the real world.

  • AI

    Applied AI, done privately.

    We run our own local AI infrastructure — retrieval, document systems, automation agents, private compute — so when we advise on AI, it's from daily hands-on practice, not vendor slides.

  • Robotics

    Physical systems expertise.

    Robotics work connects hardware, sensing, CAD, fabrication, data, safety boundaries, and human workflows — and we scope all of it before a client spends real money.

  • Clients

    People and companies first.

    Small businesses, labs, manufacturers, professionals, and individuals across Michigan and remote. The measure of an engagement is whether the client is better off.
