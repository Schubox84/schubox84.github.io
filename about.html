Built from a lifetime around computers, systems, and real work.

Schubert Consulting LLC is the result of decades of hands-on technical experience: early computers, custom builds, enterprise IT, financial-sector deployments, field operations, software tooling, automation, and now AI R&D.

Founder Story

Technology has always been more than a screen.

I have been into computers since 1991, long before AI, cloud dashboards, automation platforms, and agent workflows became business buzzwords. By 1999, I was building computers, learning hardware from the inside out, troubleshooting parts, and developing the kind of practical technical instinct that only comes from hands-on work.

That early foundation still shapes how Schubert Consulting works today. I do not look at technology as one product, one app, or one platform. I look at the whole system: the physical setup, the network, the machines, the people, the workflow, the software, the data, the automation, and now the AI layer that can sit on top of it.

Path To Now

A practical technical timeline.

  • 1991

    Started with computers early.

    The interest began young: exploring computers, learning how they behaved, and becoming comfortable around systems before they were invisible parts of daily business.

  • 1999

    Building and troubleshooting machines.

    Building computers taught the physical side of technology: parts, failure points, performance, compatibility, diagnostics, and the patience required to make hardware reliable.

  • Mid-2000s

    Enterprise IT and financial-sector contract work.

    IT contract work included support and deployment tied to large corporate and banking environments: workstation imaging, banking peripherals, user support, network troubleshooting, ticketing workflows, branch conversion support, and training groups of technicians.

  • 2010s

    Logistics, dispatch, field operations, and service pressure.

    Years in distribution, towing, dispatch, and field response showed what messy operations really look like: incomplete information, bad handoffs, customers waiting, dispatch changes, equipment readiness, and decisions made under pressure.

  • 2026

    Schubert Consulting LLC.

    The company brings those layers together: infrastructure, custom networks, software tools, automation, AI R&D, custom agents, and practical pilots that prove what works before a business commits to more.
