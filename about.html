About

Schubert Consulting LLC is now a robotics company.

The company is based in Grand Rapids, Michigan and is focused on Aerosyn: autonomous AI vehicle-inspection stations for automotive and fleet operations.

Why this pivot is fundable.

Vehicle inspection is a bounded robotics problem with clear business pain: inconsistent checks, missed evidence, slow intake, fleet readiness uncertainty, and insurance documentation gaps.

Aerosyn starts with observation and reporting, not repair. That makes the first product smaller, safer, cheaper, and easier to validate with partners.

Automotive context

The product is designed around real service workflows, not a generic robot demo.

AI systems base

Existing AI orchestration, document, retrieval, and GPU work supports the report and reasoning layer.

Operator discipline

The company can sell readiness sprints and pilots while grants fund hardware development.

The first milestone is evidence.

Partner conversations, sample inspections, a software demo, and grant-ready documentation come before a large hardware spend.
