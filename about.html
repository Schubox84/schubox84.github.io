About

Schubert Consulting LLC is the robotics and AI R&D company.

The public focus is simple: robotics, applied AI systems, prototypes, embedded sensing, and local AI infrastructure under Schubert Consulting LLC.

Operating Role

Build the runway, then build the robot.

The LLC is looking for grants and funding to continue robotics and AI research and development. It uses consulting revenue, technical materials, partner discovery, and honest prototype evidence about what is built and what is still in progress.

  • Robotics

  • Funding

    Forwarded grant, funding, compute-credit, and partner information is appreciated.
