About

Founder-led problem solving for businesses that need technology to make sense.

Schubert Consulting is built for owners and operators who know where work is getting messy but do not want an acronym-heavy sales process. The job is to understand the work first, then build the right system around it.

Schubert Consulting LLC

Custom AI is useful only when it respects the business underneath it.

The business is based in Grand Rapids, Michigan and works from a practical consulting stance: observe the process, find the friction, separate the real problem from the tool hype, and build something the company can inspect.

The goal is not to make every client sound technical. The goal is to translate daily business pressure into systems that reduce rework, clarify handoffs, and help people make better decisions with less chasing.

What You Can Expect

A direct, practical working style.

Plain language first

You should not need to speak AI to explain what is slowing your team down.

New perspective

Sometimes the useful move is seeing the workflow from outside the habits that made it normal.

Problem before platform

The best tool choice follows the bottleneck, the people, the risk, and the way the work is already done.

Ownership matters

The business should leave with maps, notes, boundaries, and a system that can be improved later.

Why Custom

One-size-fits-all AI usually fails at the handoff.

Generic AI tools can be impressive and still miss the reason your team is stuck. The important parts are often local: who approves, what data is trusted, which tool is the record, what customer promise matters, and what must never happen automatically.

Not a chatbot wrapper

The value is not a text box. The value is fitting intelligence into a real operating path.

Not a template agency

Your workflows, exceptions, files, machines, policies, and people shape what should be built.

Not pass-through work

Discovery, build direction, testing, and handoff stay accountable to the same practical goal.

Good Fit

Schubert Consulting is a fit when the problem is real but unclear.

You know work is being repeated

People copy the same information, rewrite the same messages, or hunt for the same details every week.

Your tools are useful but disconnected

The information exists, but staff still have to jump between systems to understand what is happening.

You need judgment kept in the loop

Automation should prepare and check work without removing human approval from expensive or risky decisions.

Start Small

The first conversation can be about one annoying task.

No technical brief required. If you can explain where people lose time, the first map can start there.
