How We Work

Straightforward engagements. No product, no lock-in.

Schubert Consulting LLC is hired the way any consulting firm is hired: a scoped engagement with a clear deliverable, or ongoing advisory work. Everything we build, you own.

Engagement Types

Four ways to engage.

01

Consult & assessment

A short engagement to answer a specific question: is this feasible, what will it cost, what should we do first? Delivered as a written assessment with recommendations.

02

Readiness Sprint

A fixed-scope, fixed-price start: one workflow mapped end to end, failure points identified, and the smallest useful fix recommended. $750 flat, remote.

03

Project build

A scoped implementation — an automation, an AI system, an integration, a prototype — with defined milestones, honest risk notes, and a full handoff.

04

Advisory retainer

Ongoing access to senior technical judgment: a standing monthly arrangement for organizations that want a technologist on call without a full-time hire.
