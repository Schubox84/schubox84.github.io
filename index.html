The wedge

Inspect vehicles before trying to repair them.

Aerosyn starts with a fundable robotics wedge: a vehicle moves through a fixed station while cameras, controlled lighting, OBD-II data, and AI reasoning produce a repeatable inspection report.

  • Exterior, tire, light, leak, undercarriage, and visible brake checks.
  • Human service-advisor review before customer-facing or safety-critical decisions.
  • Every reviewed inspection creates labeled data for deeper diagnostics and later manipulation.
