The Work

Robotics and AI R&D with a practical build path.

Schubert Consulting LLC starts with the useful technical middle: define the physical workflow, map the data and safety boundaries, build the smallest credible demo, and turn the idea into something testable.

  • Robotics concepts scoped around real hardware, operators, data, and constraints.
  • AI systems used for reasoning, retrieval, workflow support, and prototype acceleration.
  • Embedded sensing, edge compute, CAD, documents, and dashboards tied into one build path.
  • Milestones, constraints, risks, and test notes make the work easier to evaluate.

The Offer

What you get in a Robotics Readiness Sprint.

A bounded first engagement: map one robot, workcell, support workflow, data workflow, simulation/test path, or robot-adjacent handoff. One remote discovery call, a review of approved or sample examples, and a written picture of where to start.

Workflow map

A clear picture of how the chosen robotics workflow actually runs today, step by step.

Failure-point list

Where the workflow breaks, stalls, or creates rework, written down so it can be prioritized.

Visibility-gap list

The points where information is missing, unclear, or not captured for review.

Human-approval boundary

What must stay human-reviewed and approved, and what is safe to assist or automate.

Data/access boundary

What data and access the work needs, and what it should never touch.

Recommended first fix

The smallest useful first improvement worth doing, with the reasoning behind it.
