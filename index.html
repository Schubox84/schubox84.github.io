The Control Model

Build inside the business boundary.

The systems that decide, route, approve, update, and recover work should not be a mystery. Schubert Consulting keeps the build path close to the operator: local-first where possible, private by default, and documented enough that the company can understand what controls what.

We do the architecture, tool wrapping, workflow mapping, policy writing, QA, and handoff in house. Outside services can be connected when they are the right tool, but the control logic, runbooks, and operating boundary belong to the company.

01 Map the work

Inputs, decisions, systems touched, risks, approvals, exceptions, and recovery paths.

02 Wrap the tools

Turn apps, portals, APIs, files, and manual steps into command surfaces with readable state.

03 Gate the action

Separate read-only review, approval-required action, forbidden action, and emergency stop.

04 Leave evidence

Logs, screenshots, tests, manifests, runbooks, and handoff notes prove what happened.

Operating Rules

Ownership is the product.

No resource farming

Work is not passed through an anonymous contractor chain. The thinking, build, and QA stay inside the Schubert Consulting lane.

No black-box control

The company gets the map, policy, commands, handoff notes, and limits. The system has to be inspectable.

Human command stays first

AI prepares, summarizes, checks, and routes. People approve risky moves until the workflow earns more trust.

Evidence over theater

A good engagement leaves artifacts: manifests, logs, screenshots, tests, diagrams, runbooks, and next-step decisions.

Build Patterns

Practical control points for real companies.

Most business systems fail in the handoff: one person knows what happened, another tool has the data, and nobody owns the final decision path. These are the places to build control.

Control Point

Agent-readable service files

Public-safe llms.txt, agents.json, and OpenAPI descriptions make the company easier for assistants and operators to understand without exposing private data.

Control Point

CLI-Anything style harnesses

Human-facing tools can be wrapped with repeatable commands, JSON output, state inspection, and tests instead of fragile manual clicking.

Control Point

Dispatch and intake summaries

Calls, notes, emails, and field context become structured summaries with missing fields, uncertainty, and next actions clearly marked.

Control Point

Approval and rollback runbooks

Every risky action gets a gate, a log, a recovery path, and a plain-language explanation of who can approve it.
